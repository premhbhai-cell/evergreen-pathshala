Who We Are?
About Us

Evergreen Paathshala is an Online and Offline Film School that gives Free filmmaking learning tutorials through the Evergreen Paathshala youtube channel and offers a various low-cost distance learning IFTA Certified Audio Visual Practical courses in Modern Digital Film making and Acting & Modelling, designed and presented by producer and director Mr Samar K Mukherjee. These courses help students understand the practical roots of all crafts of filmmaking (like Screenwriting, Direction, Cinematography, Editing, Post-production, Film promotion, Publicity & Releasing, Advanced Wedding Filmmaking, etc) and also Acting & Modelling. The language of the courses is Hindi with easy English. Evergreen Paathshala also offers IFTA Certified different practical workshops, Masterclasses and Exclusive Offline Guarantee courses in Siwan and other centres for Digital Film-making. Evergreen Paathshala also conducts filmmaking seminars of Mr Samar K. Mukherjee in various universities and institutes.

"Evergreen Paathshala" and "IFTA" are a sister-concern of well-known production house "Isha Television" (Founder Mr Amiya Kumar Mukherjee) and renowned Ad Agency "Digital Dreams Creation" (driven by Mrs Shweta Mukherjee).
