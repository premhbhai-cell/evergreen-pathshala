Film Business & Distribution Mastery (FBDM)

Take Content from Production to Audience Understand how films and digital content reach audiences through publicity, promotion, marketing, distribution and exhibition. The programme also introduces the Film business side of audiovisual content—including content monetisation, advertising and revenue opportunities.
